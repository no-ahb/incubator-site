Five by Five

10 January - 28 April 2024
Artists Maggi Hambling, Jelly Green, Mona Hatoum, Tamara Al-Mashouk, Georgie Hopton, Alice McCabe, Abigail Lane, Rebecca Hancock, Ingrid Pollard, Matthew Arthur Williams

Installation views

Press release

Incubator is pleased to present Five by Five, a group exhibition showcasing the work of five established artists working in the UK, each paired with an emerging artist selected by them: Maggi Hambling & Jelly Green, Mona Hatoum & Tamara Al-Mashouk, Georgie Hopton & Alice McCabe, Abigail Lane & Rebecca Hancock, and Ingrid Pollard & Matthew Arthur Williams.

Across painting, sculpture, photography, video and installation, these ten artists explore a diverse range of themes. A range of overlapping concerns and materials among the pairings gives rise to a rich and nuanced artistic tapestry, and Five by Five stands as a testament to the power of intergenerational collaboration and mentorship.
