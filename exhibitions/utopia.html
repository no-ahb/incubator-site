Utopia

28 June - 15 September 2024
Artists Alicja Biała, Enej Gala, Laila Tara H, Ranny Macdonald, Emily Wilcock

Installation views

Press release

For Utopia, Incubator partnered with environmental law charity ClientEarth on a group exhibition addressing the most urgent and pressing issue that faces humanity: the climate crisis. The exhibition features the work of Alicja Biała, Enej Gala, Laila Tara H, Ranny Macdonald and Emily Wilcock.

Each artist created works that are not only sustainable in their materiality and process but also in their transportation to Incubator, embracing a ‘leave no trace’ philosophy. Marking the gallery’s commitment to operating as a carbon-neutral organisation, Utopia invites visitors to imagine more hopeful futures while confronting the realities of environmental breakdown.
