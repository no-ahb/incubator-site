Biography

Marius Steiger (b. 1999, Bern) is a London-based Swiss artist currently studying for an MA in Painting at the Royal College of Art.

His recent exhibitions include Values & Values with Philipp Timischl (ZIAN, Hangzhou, 2025), That Was Then, This Is Now (Jeffrey Deitch, Miami, 2025), Hermitage (Kunstraum Riehen, Basel, 2025), Clear History (Perrotin, Paris, 2025), Brick Breath Horizon (Lisson X Zian, Shanghai, 2025), Maison Clearing (CLEARING NY LA, Basel, 2025), and Monsieur Hulot (Blue Velvet, Zurich, 2025).
