Biography

Eva Dixon (b. 2000, Australia) lives and works in London. Dixon received their BA in Fine Art from Central Saint Martin's (2023) and is attending the Royal Academy from October 2026. Recent solo presentations include All-American (2026) at Art Cologne Palma with Particular Ideas, SCORE! at Split Rivera (2025), Mercury 13 at WIP Space (2025), Lesbian Trucker Paintings at The Fores Project (2023). Notable group exhibitions include 'What Holds Things Together' CC Jasper, New York, Preview art fair with Caption Seoul (Korea, 2026), Lands End (London, Art Mama, 2024) and duo exhibition Top Dog at Parlour Gallery (London, 2026), Rose Easton (London, 2025), Saatchi Gallery (London 2023, 2025), Christie's (London, 2023). Dixon is a recipient of the Maison/0 This Earth award with LVMH (2023) and 'Best UK solo presentation' at Minor Attractions (2025, Split Rivera).
