Biography

Atticus Wakefield (b. 1998, New York) received his BA in 2020 from UCLA, with a focus on painting and art history.

Wakefield has participated in group exhibitions at Estrella Gallery, New York (2021) (Death of Ulysses); Gaaaaasp, New York (2021) (Two Left Feet); Anonymous Gallery, New York (2022) (Bermuda Triangle); and Ambar Quijano, Mexico City (2022) (Liquid Frictions).

The Song Remains the Same at Incubator 23 is Wakefield’s first solo exhibition.
