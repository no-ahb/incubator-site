Biography

Harry has recently had a solo show at Haarlem Art Space in September 2025. In 2024 he showed at Alice Amati, Slugtown and Unit 1 Gallery. In Summer 2023 Harry presented his first public commissions for the Chester Contemporary in the UK. He completed a residency at Palazzo Monti in Brescia, Italy in July 2023. Harry has created sculptural editions for Hi Noon Editions and Object Multiple and a book of poems with Saltstung Press. He has guest tutored at Kingston University and London College of Communication. Harry has written for Elephant Magazine, AIGA, and Creative Lives in Progress. He graduated from Kingston School of Art in 2016 with first class honours in graphic design and trained as a clown at Albert and Friends Circus School in 2007.
