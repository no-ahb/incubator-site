Biography

Losel Yauch is a Tibetan-American painter and textile artist. Born and raised in New York City, Yauch moved to London to complete her BA at Central Saint Martins, where she received the New Futures Graduate Art Prize upon graduating in 2022.

Her recent exhibitions include At This Hour, La Loma, Los Angeles (2024); Wells Art Contemporary, Somerset (2022); From Oblivion, With Love, Incubator (2022); Saatchi Art’s The Other Art Fair, London (2022); and Lazy Susan Gallery, New York City (2019).
