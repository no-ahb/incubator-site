Biography

Mary Stephenson (b. 1989) is the first of six artists exhibiting as part of the Incubator 22 programme. Born and raised in London, Stephenson completed her MFA at the Royal Academy of Arts.

Her recent exhibitions include Hue (with Dirk Stewen) at Maureen Paley, London (2026); Mary! Go Round at White Cube, Paris (2025); For Crude Hints (Towards) at Ginny on Frederick, Frieze 9 Cork Street, London (2025); Abigail’s Party at Rose Easton, London (2025); Heart Throbs at Chapter Gallery, New York (2024); Soft Footing at MASSIMODECARLO, Paris (2024); and With Urgency at Ilenia Gallery, London (2024).
