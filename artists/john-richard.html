Biography

John Richard is a photographer whose work captures the ephemeral beauty of the natural world. Having built a career in portraiture, he has recently turned his focus to landscapes, dedicating the past three years to photographing the sea at dawn.

His images distill fleeting moments of an ever-moving world, creating still-frame compositions that convey both serenity and perpetual motion.
