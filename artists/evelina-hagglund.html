Biography

Evelina Hägglund lives and works between London and Stockholm. After completing her bachelor’s degree at the Academy of Visual Arts in Ljubljana in 2018, she graduated from the MFA programme at Goldsmiths, London, in 2021.

Recent exhibitions include Exit From Within at Ditte Lauridsen, Stockholm (2026), I Dreamt I Was the Ground at Golsa, Oslo (2024), and From Birth to Earth at Parafin, London (2023). She has also exhibited in at one with the world at Incubator, London (2022), somewhere I have never travelled, gladly beyond at Nicoletti Contemporary, London (2022), and II (framed) at Saatchi Gallery, London (2021).
