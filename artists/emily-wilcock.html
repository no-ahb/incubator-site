Biography

Emily Wilcock (b. 2001, Brisbane) is a British-Australian artist in her final year studying Fine Art at Camberwell College of Arts.

Her recent exhibitions include Peculiar Child at Asylum Chapel, London (2023), and Half Way at A.P.T. Gallery, London (2022).
