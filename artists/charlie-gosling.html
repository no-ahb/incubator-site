Biography

Charlie Gosling is a London-based artist whose practice focuses on portraiture, exploring how a subject’s peculiarities can act as windows into their inner world. Born and raised in Hackney, he graduated Fine Art at Camberwell College of Arts in 2023.

Painting the skew of a tie with the same devotion as the lips of a loved one, Gosling’s work reflects a sustained interest in capturing the quirks of his subjects—creating portraits that are lighthearted in style yet strikingly ambitious in their intent.

Recent exhibitions include Just Kids at Palo Gallery, New York (2024), BeauBeaus X Bomb Factory Workshop (London, 2024), Cut Out at HM Electric Gallery (London, 2023), Lines in Sand at Incubator (London, 2023), and Whose Muse? at Palo Gallery (New York, 2022).
